# A short Niu Lai demonstration for proposal reviewers

Allow about five minutes. Open the live app and use **Start a new demo** if needed.
The application demonstrates the supplied Niu Lai packet and authored reference
judgments. It makes no live AI calls. Approval buttons record visitor progress;
they do not establish independent expert validation.

| Screen | Click | Point to explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Step 1: Preserve the puzzle | Open an evidence card, then **Approve puzzle** | Separate the reported pattern from an explanation. Keep source-verification limits and rival descriptions visible. |
| Step 2: Map the theoretical search space | **Approve literature map** | The retained theory distinguishes awareness from evaluation. This is a curated source; source discovery and exhaustive coverage are not demonstrated. |
| Step 3: Lock the expectation | Inspect the permitted input, then **Load reference expectation** and **Approve and lock expectation** | A qualified expectation is grounded in the source. The adapter's input excludes focal evidence and the downstream survival review. A challenge pass checks the temptation to predict unconditional commercial decline. |
| Step 4: Assess the relationship | **Load relationship assessment**, then **Approve compatibility assessment** | The reported commercial direction is compatible with the qualified expectation. The workflow does not force a contradiction. Individual purchaser motives remain unresolved. |
| Step 5: Propose and test | **Load candidate reconstruction**, then **Lock reconstruction and proposed tests** | Participatory value is a candidate mechanism. The app records rival accounts, observations against it, and prospective comparisons that could distinguish it from awareness and access. |
| Completion | **Open survival review** | Transfer remains **NOT EVALUATED** because no independent held-out observations were supplied. |
| Survival review | **Run authored survival review**, then open a replacement derivation | The five qualified outcomes have authored alternative derivations after joint removal of A1–A5 under the retained corpus. Earlier replacement outcomes feed later steps at their stated scope. |
| Sources & audit | Inspect approved records and events; download the audit ZIP | The conceptual claims can be traced to inputs, approvals, versioned records, and review arguments. |

The survival screen reports **5 / 5 qualified outcomes** in the authored review
and **1 / 11 removal scenarios assessed**. The other ten scenarios remain
unassessed. This result is conditional on retaining the supplied case evidence
and curated theory note. It is not an observed unaided researcher performance,
a claim of zero practical AI benefit, or a percentage of authorship.

For a particularly useful example, open **I5 / R5**. Its premises motivate a
research question and discriminating test. They do not establish that the
participatory mechanism caused the reported commercial outcome.

The app fixes the scientific inputs and authored outputs so they continue to
match the supplied reference-input hashes. It is a guided reference replay,
not an interface for entering new case evidence or freely changing judgments.
Alternative empirical assessments require a separately reviewed case or
assessment form using the existing command-line tools.

Download the workflow report and complete audit ZIP. After running survival,
that ZIP also contains the review plan, assessments, summary, and review report.
Use the downloadable reports as a fallback during a live presentation.
