# Analysis of sequential survival

The principal output follows each removal from puzzle to reconstruction. At each step, show the target, retained premises, alternative derivation, established scope, survival judgment, and consequences for later steps. Where original process histories exist, add the AI input, human decision, and observed change.

## Outcomes and quality

`recovered_equivalent` means a substantively equivalent outcome is supported under the retained inputs. `weakened` means only a narrower or less supported outcome is established. `not_recovered_within_budget` requires a completed bounded search or replay. `indeterminate` records insufficient support, unclear independence, or an incomplete assessment.

A missing original checkpoint does not automatically make survival indeterminate. A newly constructed retrospective derivation may establish survival while historical discovery remains unknown. The same distinction applies when the evaluator knows the target.

Keep the exact replacement outcome in the record. Downstream judgments must use its actual scope. A missing route in the original graph does not establish non-survival: the review can introduce a new route from retained premises. The software checks branch membership and step order, while substantive independence requires human review.

Assess quality without the contribution as `improves`, `unchanged`, `worsens`, or `indeterminate`, with reasons. Survival does not imply zero benefit: AI may improve justification, search efficiency, or attention. Actual efficiency gains require observations; they are not inferred from survival counts.

## Descriptive summaries

Report counts of surviving, weakened, unrecovered, indeterminate, and unassessed outcomes for each removal, plus the final-step result. The survival fraction is defined only when the full registered set is coded, full-record support is known, and each supported outcome has a determinate survival judgment. Its denominator is the supported target-outcome set. Report missing judgments explicitly.

The separate finite non-recovery fraction additionally requires bounded search or replay assessments for every supported outcome. Retrospective positive derivations can establish survival without being treated as search trials. Neither fraction measures the percentage of a paper created by AI. Individual, group, and cumulative removals overlap and must not be added as shares of credit.

The Niu Lai review reports five qualified surviving outcomes in an authored, fixed-corpus assessment. This is a demonstration of the reasoning procedure. It is not an estimate of zero practical AI benefit, historical necessity, independent human performance, or transfer success.

## Negative findings and deviations

Retain unsupported claims, rejected suggestions, new insights after removal, and improvements in the alternative account. Record missing dependencies, changed source availability, failed calls, and revisions to removal rules. Changes to the eligible corpus create a different backtest boundary and must be stated. Do not convert historical uncertainty into a negative survival result.
