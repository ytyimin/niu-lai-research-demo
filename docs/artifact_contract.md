# Artifact contract

The proposal, appendix, and repository form one package. The evaluation asks whether a step’s outcome survives removal of AI contributions under declared inputs, then follows the consequences through reconstruction. Historical discovery attribution is a separate judgment.

## Protected workflow

The original workflow preserves puzzle approval, literature selection, the Step 3 information boundary, expectation locking, human theory–evidence adjudication, reconstruction locking, and prospective transfer. The synthetic example reaches export. The Niu Lai workflow stops at reconstruction locking because it has no independent transfer evidence.

## Sequential survival

`backtest.py` freezes the case ledger and source hashes, validates the dependency graph, and computes old-record exclusions for individual, joint, and cumulative removals. A missing old route does not imply non-attainability.

The assessment form permits retrospective replacement derivations. `derivation_id` identifies the new argument; `replacement_outcome` states its exact conclusion and scope; `retained_input_ids` lists direct premises. `upstream_derivation_ids` links to supported earlier replacements on the same branch, and `upstream_outcomes_used` preserves their exact scope.

The validator rejects excluded premises, unknown or forward dependencies, cross-branch imports, and silent changes to upstream outcomes. Missing history does not block a supported retrospective derivation. An unresolved upstream judgment cannot support a later argument, but a separate direct route remains assessable.

`survival_demo.py` binds the authored Niu Lai review to a frozen plan and generates a readable report. Its five qualified outcomes survive under the declared case-and-theory corpus. This demonstrates an argument and its bookkeeping; independent reviewers must assess its substantive adequacy. The final outcome is a candidate mechanism and proposed test, not a causal finding.

## Limits of verification

The software checks structure and consistency. It does not prove source truth, semantic equivalence, independence concealed in prose, or completeness of search. An evaluator can know the target, but the target cannot serve as its own premise. Retrospective assessment does not replace the prospective Step 3 input restriction.

The store is append-only through its API, not tamper-proof storage. Complete live process capture and configured computational removal replays remain development work. Empty forms do not generate results. Authored reviews, observed assessments, and software fixtures retain distinct labels.
